# The component stand

Every component of the workspace, rendered on its own with fixed data, next to its documentation. Use it to
see what already exists before building something new, to try a component in both themes and three
languages, and to check a change without clicking through the app.

## What is here

**Design tokens** documents `packages/ui/src/theme.css`: colors, typography, radii, breakpoints. Everything else
is a component, listed below with the first sentence of its description. The list is read from the story files,
so it is always complete.

## Reading a component's page

Each component has a **Docs** page, built from the component's own file:

- **The path under the title** is where the component lives. The UI kit is imported by name
  (`import { Button } from '@lc/ui'`); a widget or a feature is internal to its module and is used by that
  module's page.
- **The description** is the JSDoc comment above the component.
- **The first story with its controls**: change a prop in the table and the story follows. Big objects such as
  the plan have no control.
- **The table of props**: name, type, default and the comment above the prop in its interface. A prop marked
  with a red asterisk is required.
- **Every story**, each a state worth knowing about, with a line on what to look at. "Show code" under a story
  gives the JSX that renders it.

## The toolbar

- **Theme**: light or dark. It rewrites `<meta name="color-scheme">`, the same switch the app uses.
- **Language**: ru, en, es. Interface text comes from `@lc/i18n`; the plan itself (event titles, course
  names) stays Russian, as in the app.
- **Accessibility** (panel at the bottom): axe checks of the story on screen.
- **Interactions** (panel at the bottom): stories with a `play` function click through a scenario and assert
  the result.

`pnpm run stand:test` runs both for every story in a headless browser.

## How the workspace is layered

```
apps/web, apps/stand ──▶ modules/* ──▶ packages/plan-domain
   │                         └───────▶ packages/ui, packages/i18n
   └── apps/web only ──▶ packages/plan-data ──▶ packages/plan-domain
```

- **`packages/ui`** knows nothing about the plan: plain controls and the theme.
- **`packages/plan-domain`** is the plan in pure TypeScript: types, dates, schedule rules, edits.
- **`modules/*`** are the product, split by Feature-Sliced layers. A **widget** shows a part of the plan and
  reports what the person did through callbacks. A **feature** is one user action, usually a dialog. A
  **page** composes them and owns the state.
- **Apps** compose modules. A module never loads data or saves it: the app passes the plan in and receives
  every edit as one `PlanChange` value.

That last rule is why every component here works without a server: a story passes a plan and a callback,
exactly as the app does. The full rules are in `docs/architecture.md`.

## The data in the stories

All stories share one plan from `@lc/plan-domain/fixtures`: the real schedule, built in memory by
`buildPlan()`, with "now" fixed at Wednesday, 2 December 2026, 12:40. Nothing reads `packages/plan-data`,
so the stories do not change when the plan is edited in the app. The planner page story is the exception for
the clock: it follows the real time, like the app.

## Adding a story

Put `Name.stories.tsx` next to `Name.tsx`. See `docs/component-stand.md` for a template.
